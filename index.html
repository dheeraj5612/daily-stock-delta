HOW TO READ THE RANKING

Asymmetry needs evidence.

Order follows supported valuation anomalies, probability-weighted risk/reward, and earnings or catalyst torque over 12–24 months. Each name carries its own reasoning and source status. An early research lead can appear before valuation work is complete. Missing scenarios stay marked unavailable; a high position is not a Buy rating.
